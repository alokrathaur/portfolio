## Design Context

### Users
The portfolio targets three primary audiences: potential employers/recruiters looking to hire iOS developers, fellow developers interested in the technical work showcased, and potential clients seeking freelance iOS development services. Additionally, it serves users interested in playing the DotMesh game showcased in the portfolio.

### Brand Personality
Professional & polished with undertones of creative & innovative. The portfolio balances corporate acceptability suitable for job applications with personality that showcases the developer's passion for building engaging apps and games.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark & vibrant theme - the current design already establishes this direction with dark backgrounds and accent colors. The aesthetic should maintain a sophisticated, tech-forward feel while incorporating subtle gaming-inspired elements from the DotMesh project to show personality without compromising professionalism.

### Design Principles
1. **Maintain professional credibility** - Design choices should enhance, not detract from, the perception of a senior iOS developer
2. **Showcase personality through details** - Subtle gaming-inspired elements in appropriate sections (like project showcases)
3. **Prioritize clarity and scannability** - Information should be quickly digestible for recruiters scanning portfolios
4. **Maintain visual consistency** - Consistent spacing, typography, and color usage throughout
5. **Highlight accomplishments** - Metrics and achievements should be visually prominent and easy to digest
